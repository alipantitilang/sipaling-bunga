# FLOWER INTERPRETATION

Flower meaning is context, not authority.

Semantic indicators may include:
- visual_attraction
- emotional_connection
- personal_experience
- symbolic_connection
- cultural_connection
- media_influence
- memory_connection
- philosophical_connection
- chance_encounter
- identity_connection
- resilience
- hope
- self_reflection
- personal_growth
- appearance_vs_meaning

These are indicators, not rankings.

The same flower can carry different stories for different Gardeners.
